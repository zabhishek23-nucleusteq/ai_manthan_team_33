Severity: SEV3 (authorization checked after validation). Steps: login as employee@expenseflow.test, POST /api/admin/users with body {} (also PUT /api/admin/users/{id}/roles with {}). Expected: 403 FORBIDDEN (rbac.md s3: wrong role -> 403). Actual: 400, because @PreAuthorize on the method runs after @RequestBody binding/validation, so unauthorized callers see validation errors. Fix: authorize before binding, e.g. requestMatchers for /api/admin/** with hasRole SUPER_ADMIN in SecurityConfig (keep @PreAuthorize as defence in depth). Evidence: tests/e2e/api/rbac.spec.ts and the e2e-api CI job on PR 34. Regression test: remove the KNOWN_BUGS entries in rbac.spec.ts.
