payouts_total{outcome=failure} is registered lazily at the first failure (value 1), so increase(...[5m]) sees no rise and the alert stays inactive (L4 rehearsal 2026-11-27: one failure, 5+ min, no alert; two failures -> fired at 85 s). Fix: pre-register payouts_total for outcome=success|failure at 0 in BankCallMetrics on startup. Workaround in docs/demo-script.md: fail the submit twice.
