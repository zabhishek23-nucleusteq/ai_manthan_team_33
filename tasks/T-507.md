Severity: SEV3 (OWASP A07 identification and authentication failures). Steps: POST /api/auth/login 25 times with a valid email and wrong password. Expected: throttling (429 TOO_MANY_REQUESTS, the frontend already maps 429 to a message) or temporary lockout. Actual: 25 x 401, then the correct password still works immediately, so passwords can be brute-forced at full speed. Fix: simple in-memory limiter per email+IP (e.g. 5 failures per minute -> 429 with Retry-After), keep the generic error body. Evidence: manual probe in docs/testing/security-checklist.md.
